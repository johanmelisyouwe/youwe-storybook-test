# Youwe design system

This Storybook mirrors the Youwe Figma file. **Figma is the source of truth**: components, variants, tokens and page templates are built from Figma, and updated by running the Figma → Storybook sync (see `FIGMA-SYNC.md` in the repo).

## What's here

- **Foundations**: colours, typography, spacing, radius, motion and effects. From Figma variables & styles → `tokens/figma-variables.json` → `npm run tokens`.
- **Components**: buttons, cards, inputs… with every Figma variant as a story. From Figma component sets.
- **Content blocks**: page sections such as hero, card grid, case carousel and stats. From the Figma “Content blocks/…” components.
- **Templates**: approved page templates (desktop + mobile). From the Figma Templates page, frames named `Page / …`.

## Toolbar

- **Mode**: dark / light, the two modes of the Figma `color_semantics` collection.
- **Viewport**: Mobile (360) and Desktop (1440) match the Figma frame widths.
- **Accessibility**: every story is checked for contrast and labels (a11y panel).

## Fonts

Aktiv Grotesk (Regular, Medium, Bold), the family and weights the Figma text styles use, served from `public/fonts/aktiv-grotesk/`.
